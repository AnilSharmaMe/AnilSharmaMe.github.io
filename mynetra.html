Mynetra Pty Ltd

Software built with privacy first

Mynetra is an Australian software and technology company. We design and build consumer mobile apps and cloud-based platforms that help businesses reach and look after their customers, and we are upfront about what data our products handle and why.

What we do

We take a product from idea to the App Store and Google Play: the app, the servers, the data feeds and the privacy and security work in between.

Mobile apps

Native iPhone and Android apps, built for everyday use and for the moments when an app has to work immediately, such as a safety alert or a family check-in.

Cloud and real-time services

Secure back-end services that move information quickly: live data feeds, push notifications to many devices at once, and location-aware logic that only sends what is relevant.

On-device intelligence

Where it makes sense, machine learning runs on the phone itself, so sensitive data such as photos stays with the person who owns it instead of travelling to a server.

Privacy and security by design

Encrypted connections, device verification of requests, minimal data collection and plain-language policies. We say what is private and what is not, and we do not sell personal information.

Our products

One live consumer app, and a new business platform.

Real-time safety alerts from official emergency channels and your community, private family location sharing with one-tap alerts, and on-device face matching against public watchlists. Free to start, with an optional Spotter Plus subscription.
