Privacy, plainly explained.

We'd rather tell you exactly what happens to your data than make vague promises. Here's the honest breakdown.

On-Device Face Matching

Face comparison runs entirely on your device using CoreML. Photos and face data are never uploaded — matching happens locally, against public wanted/missing-persons watchlists or your own private list, and nothing leaves your phone.

Encrypted in Transit, Approval-Gated

Family location updates travel over encrypted (TLS) connections and are only ever visible to family members who've explicitly accepted your invite. We don't sell location data or share it with third parties. We're not claiming zero-knowledge here — the server needs to route locations to approved family members to make live sharing work at all — and we'd rather say that plainly than overstate it.

Our Mission

Spotter was born out of a simple idea: communities should be able to protect each other without sacrificing their privacy. We believe technology can help neighborhoods stay informed and families stay connected, built by being upfront about what data we handle and why — not by overselling what "private" means.

Questions people actually ask.

Straight answers, including the hard ones.

Why does a safety app need face search — isn't that creepy?

It's not a general "search anyone" tool. Face matching only runs against closed, named lists — public wanted/missing-persons registries, or your own private list — never a general web or social-media search. Comparison happens entirely on your device; photos are never uploaded anywhere. That's a deliberate design choice, not a limitation we're working around.

Family location tracking can be misused for stalking or domestic abuse. What do you do about that?

Every connection requires mutual approval — no one can add you without your consent. You can pause sharing, or switch to an approximate suburb-level location instead of your exact position, at any time. Removing a connection takes effect immediately and isn't announced to the removed person, so leaving quietly doesn't tip anyone off. It's not a perfect solution to a hard problem, but it's one we keep working on.

Is my data really private? What's actually encrypted?

We'd rather be precise than reassuring. Face data never leaves your device — that part is genuinely zero-knowledge. Family location is transmitted over an encrypted (TLS) connection and only shared with people you've approved, but it isn't end-to-end encrypted — our server needs to route it to make live sharing work. See the Security section and our Privacy Policy for the full breakdown.

Why not just use VicEmergency or other official apps?

You should still use them — Spotter isn't a replacement for official emergency channels, and we say that explicitly in the app. What Spotter adds is bringing multiple official feeds (VicEmergency, stolen vehicle registers, missing persons) together with family safety tools in one place, instead of checking several separate sources.

Who's actually behind this?

Spotter is built and maintained by an independent developer based in Australia — not a government body. Questions or concerns are welcome any time at admin@spotterapp.com.au.
