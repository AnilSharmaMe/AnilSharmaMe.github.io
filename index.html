Safety in everyday life.

Six ordinary moments where Spotter quietly does its job.

Risk Radar

Saturday at the market

You drive to a market you have never been to. As you reach the area, your phone buzzes: “High risk detected: 6 car thefts reported nearby, in the last 3 days.”

You park on the busy street instead and take your bag with you. Nothing was said by a stranger, and you never opened the app.

Saved places

Did the kids get to school?

You save Home and School once. At 8:42 every morning your family sees “Anju arrived at School”, and at 3:50 “Anju left School”.

No “are you there yet?” texts. Whoever is at work knows, and so does Nana, if you chose to tell her.

Family near an alert

A bushfire near Mum

A fire starts near the town where Mum lives. Spotter shows the alert and says “Mum is 450 m from this”.

One tap asks “Are you OK?”. It only speaks when her location is recent and shared with you, so it never guesses.

Alert my family

Walking home late

The last train was late and the street is dark. You tap one button and your family circle gets a message with your location.

No menus, no typing. Free for everyone, always.

Names

Mum, Dad, Sam

You call them what you call them. Notifications use your names too: “Dad arrived at Work”, not a username.

Only you see the names you give, and each person chooses how their own family sees them.

Area Insights

Choosing where to rent

You are choosing between two suburbs. The heat map shows where car thefts and other incidents have clustered over the last months.

You see the pattern instead of one alert. Locations are approximate, never an exact pin.

These are illustrative scenarios showing how each feature behaves, not customer stories. Spotter only mentions where someone is when they have chosen to share it with you.

📡 Risk Radar NEW

Spotter keeps watch in the background, comparing the places you travel through with real incident reports: car thefts, accidents, fires and more. If you are about to enter an area where something has been happening, you get a heads-up, even when the app is closed, so you can take precautions before you arrive. Quiet until it matters, and at most one warning per area a week.

Spot Flares

Alert your community instantly about local hazards and incidents. Drop a flare, and notify everyone nearby in milliseconds. The live feed shows how far each alert is from the people you care about.

On-Device AI

Scan against your secure vault entirely on-device using advanced CoreML. No cloud processing means total privacy and instant results.

Family Map

Keep track of your loved ones on a private map, and call them what you call them: Mum, Dad, Sam. Invites need approval on both sides. Pause sharing anytime, or share an approximate, suburb-level location instead of your exact position.

Know When Family Is Near an Alert NEW

If someone you love is within 1 km of a fire or accident, Spotter tells you, with their distance, and you can ask “Are you OK?” in one tap. Only when their location is recent and shared with you, never a guess.

One-Tap Safety Alert

Send an instant alert with a custom message to your family circle when you need help — no fumbling through menus in an urgent moment.

Everyone sees how everyone else is doing without a single phone call. A recent-activity list lets you look back, you choose who is told for each place, and nothing is sent while sharing is paused.

Privacy, plainly explained.

We'd rather tell you exactly what happens to your data than make vague promises. Here's the honest breakdown.

On-Device Face Matching

Face comparison runs entirely on your device using CoreML. Photos and face data are never uploaded — matching happens locally, against public wanted/missing-persons watchlists or your own private list, and nothing leaves your phone.

Encrypted in Transit, Approval-Gated

Family location updates travel over encrypted (TLS) connections and are only ever visible to family members who've explicitly accepted your invite. We don't sell location data or share it with third parties. We're not claiming zero-knowledge here — the server needs to route locations to approved family members to make live sharing work at all — and we'd rather say that plainly than overstate it.

Our Mission

Spotter was born out of a simple idea: communities should be able to protect each other without sacrificing their privacy. We believe technology can help neighborhoods stay informed and families stay connected, built by being upfront about what data we handle and why — not by overselling what "private" means.

Questions people actually ask.

Straight answers, including the hard ones.

Why does a safety app need face search — isn't that creepy?

It's not a general "search anyone" tool. Face matching only runs against closed, named lists — public wanted/missing-persons registries, or your own private list — never a general web or social-media search. Comparison happens entirely on your device; photos are never uploaded anywhere. That's a deliberate design choice, not a limitation we're working around.

Family location tracking can be misused for stalking or domestic abuse. What do you do about that?

Every connection requires mutual approval — no one can add you without your consent. You can pause sharing, or switch to an approximate suburb-level location instead of your exact position, at any time. Removing a connection takes effect immediately and isn't announced to the removed person, so leaving quietly doesn't tip anyone off. It's not a perfect solution to a hard problem, but it's one we keep working on.

Is my data really private? What's actually encrypted?

We'd rather be precise than reassuring. Face data never leaves your device — that part is genuinely zero-knowledge. Family location is transmitted over an encrypted (TLS) connection and only shared with people you've approved, but it isn't end-to-end encrypted — our server needs to route it to make live sharing work. See the Security section and our Privacy Policy for the full breakdown.

Why not just use VicEmergency or other official apps?

You should still use them — Spotter isn't a replacement for official emergency channels, and we say that explicitly in the app. What Spotter adds is bringing multiple official feeds (VicEmergency, stolen vehicle registers, missing persons) together with family safety tools in one place, instead of checking several separate sources.

Is Spotter really free?

Yes — right now every feature in Spotter is free for everyone: safety alerts, unlimited family members, unlimited Spot face searches, full alert history, Risk Radar and Area Insights. Spotter Plus, an optional paid plan, may return in future; if it does, we'll say so clearly in the app first. Nothing is ever paywalled mid-emergency — SOS and safety alerts are free, full stop. If you already subscribed to Spotter Plus, your subscription continues until you cancel it in your Apple ID or Google Play settings.
